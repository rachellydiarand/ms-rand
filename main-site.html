I often feel like I'm in the ending scenes of the movie Contact. The trial. Trying to "prove it". Trying to figure out what is possible and what is not.

I know I've been tortured by others for decades. In all kinds of ways.

I went to the VA hospital for help getting off of the streets. I suppose it doesn't make sense to go to a hospital for that, but I had nowhere else to go. It's still the same story.

I get mad at all of this. How me and apparently my friends and family have been treated.

When my medical record states "poor insight", when I'm trying to educate myself in a new career to get away from the art career of music which I do not enjoy the work of. The groups. My ideas now are that they have some education that I do not have. They are holding some religious world order thing over me.

I simply do not understand all of this. I don't understand how people can PURPOSLY torture other people.

But I'm pretty old. Almost 75% complete with my life if I live the average amount of time. If people don't want me as president, and from my contributor list, they apparently do not, then I simply want out of the business so I can do my computer coding and music. All solitary. Just like my 40 year meditation practice.

The best way for me to make that clean break is for people to email me: rachel.lydia.rand@gmail.com with some kind words of "It's OK, we just don't want you as president Rachel. We're not going to kill you. You can do your retirement work now. You did your best and that's all anyone can do, even if the world needs more from us."

Some kind of confirmation of failure if not confirmation of success. I enjoy the political work. It's amazing! But if people are just going to actively hate me on the streets, then it's only natural to want them to stop.

The sound of the boulevard out here is crazy bad. I'd rather live next to an airport at this point in life. I've lived on a boulevard or freeway for 17 years of my adult life.

All this anger may be about my gender/sex change. But then again, this has been going on my entire life. I simply do not know what it is about. My life story is stacked with high level connections yet I'm all alone. And I can hardly imagine not being alone. I don't know that I could handle not being alone.

There was a lady in my old neighborhood who had a husky dog who she would walk at 4am. Of course I saw her many times because I enjoy walking then too. I could not get within a block of her. The dog would bark and she would hold it back and turn the other direction. I'm the same way. She's my people. And the morning joggers of course.

To find peace, so that I can get back to non-presidential work, I need to move. To a place closer to a grocery store that has the things I enjoy eating. Somewhere not directly on the busiest freeway west of the Mississippi. Somewhere with the least amount of churches and bars possible.

I can be friendly. I really enjoy being friendly. But I'm still an introvert and always will be. If people don't want me the way I am, then they just don't want me. I keep on feeling like people are torturing me because they want me to change. They may be.

I also wish to die alone when my time comes. I keep on thinking of a room like a jail cell. With a drain in the middle. Just lock the door and let me die like a moth. Patty kind of died like that. I loved her for that. The best years of my life were with her.

I'm not much of a group musician. I only slightly enjoyed playing in the Memphis Jazz Orchestra. I enjoy tell the exotic stories.

I've likely played my bass using the electronic pickups about 5% of the time over my entire life. The last bass amplifier I had was in 1999. Although I use my studio monitors and headphones, most of the time I just pick the bass up and play it.

I threw about a pound of gold in the dumpster a few minutes ago. Is that like $50,000? It's likely not much of anything these days, right? It's not money.

Sorry if this bums people out. Believe me, I'm not too excited about it either. I'm not a musician president that most people would think a musician president would be. I don't write lyrics. I started singing at age 50. I've played guitar for about 4 years in my life. I play 12 different musical instruments.

Church culture is completely foreign to me. It's like poison to me. So is alcohol for the most part. And bars. It's how I was raised. It disturbs me to see notations in my medical record about my dad being an alcoholic or heavy drinker. I never saw anyone in my family drink or say a prayer. We didn't even sing happy birthday because we all had the same birthday.

Likely planned to be that way. But for what purpose? Well, why don't you email me your theories?
